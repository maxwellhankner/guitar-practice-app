# Development roadmap

## Songs

1. Verse, chorus, picking, and bridge sections
2. Save a song and open it later

## Keys

1. Clearer top match when finding a key
2. Tests for scoring, transpose, and Roman numerals

## Fretboard

1. Several fingerings per chord
2. Choose a fingering on each step
3. Shapes higher up the neck
4. Capo on the diagram
5. Left-handed fretboard

## Additional

1. Be able to play and have the app listen, guess the key, record the notes, show you the scale on fretboard, show where the current noise is being played
2. Show the fretboard positions when playing a progression through. Show finger picking portions as well. 
3. Distinguish picking notes, power/partial chords, and chords. Use all the building blocks in a single song or progression.
